PREFACE & INTENT

A Little About Us

A collection of three people’s stories, personalities, memories, and shared experiences.

Welcome to our little corner of the web. If you have spent any time browsing modern screens, you know how noisy everything can feel—fast, transient, and forgotten by morning. We wanted something different.

This website is an intimate anthology. It gathers the distinct personalities, memories, curiosities, and quiet work of three individuals: Charan, Aakash, and Sanjay Kumar. We come from different backgrounds and hold different passions, but we share a common belief that every human life is a book worth reading slowly.

Whether you are here out of curiosity, a shared love for vintage literature, or simply passing through, we invite you to sit for a moment and turn these digital pages.

“We believe that what is written down with honesty is never truly lost to time. Here are our fragments of memory, ink, and purpose.”

HISTORICAL TIMELINE

How Our Stories Became One

A chronicle of how three independent paths converged into a single shared voyage.

❦   ❦   ❦
01
CHAPTER I • BEFORE THE CROSSROADS

Three Lives, Different Directions

Before Chennai became a shared destination, our stories were unfolding in different cities. Aakash and Sanjay were completing their college journeys, preparing for the next chapter of their lives. Charan, meanwhile, had already stepped into the professional world of graphic design, discovering his creative side before deciding to explore frontend development. Three different beginnings, each carrying its own ambitions.

02
CHAPTER II • A NEW DIRECTION

The Decision to Learn Something New

College was coming to an end for Aakash and Sanjay, bringing with it the question of what comes next. Somewhere along the way, technology caught their attention, and the decision to explore development began taking shape. Charan was already familiar with the creative side of digital work, but wanted to understand how those visual ideas could come alive through code. Eventually, all three found themselves moving towards the same destination: learning frontend development.

03
CHAPTER III • THE MEETING POINT

Besant Technologies, Anna Nagar

A learning space in Anna Nagar, Chennai, became the place where three separate journeys finally crossed. At Besant Technologies, we came together as students of the same frontend development course. Different hometowns, different experiences, and different perspectives slowly turned unfamiliar faces into familiar company. What initially brought us together was a course; what kept us connected was the shared experience of learning something new.

04
CHAPTER IV • THE SHARED CANVAS

Learning Alongside Each Other

As the days passed, lessons became conversations, small doubts became shared discoveries, and individual learning slowly turned into a collective experience. Charan brought his background in visual design, while Aakash and Sanjay began building their understanding of the world of development. Each arrived with a different starting point, but all shared the same willingness to experiment, improve, and figure things out together.

05
CHAPTER V • THE FIRST COLLECTIVE PAGE

Three Stories, One Beginning

Somewhere between learning tags, styling layouts, exchanging ideas, and figuring out how the web works, the idea of creating something together took shape. This biography website became our first shared canvas — a small project that carries more than code. It holds three beginnings, three perspectives, and a reminder of where our paths first came together. We may still be at the beginning of our journeys, but this is one chapter worth remembering.
